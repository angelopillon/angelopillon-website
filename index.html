The hybrid chain

Plugins do the precise work. A few analog stages do what plugins only approximate: glue, weight, and the way a mix holds together when it gets loud.

Digital first. Corrective EQ, dynamics, automation and space are done in the box with UAD, FabFilter and Waves, where every move can be recalled.

The analog stages are chosen per track, not stacked by habit. Then the final stage: true peak limiting to −0.3 or −0.2 dBTP, loudness set for where the record is going, and every delivery format you need.

How a project runs

  1. Send your files

    Stems or a stereo mix as WAV, with the tempo and a short note about the track. I check everything before I start.

  2. Brief and references

    Tell me how it should feel and where it is going: reference tracks, platforms, club or streaming.

  3. Mix and master

    The session runs through the hybrid chain. You get a first version with notes on what I changed and why.

  4. Revisions and delivery

    Ask for any change. When it is right you receive the final files for streaming, club and download.

Questions

Anything else, ask in the form below.

How do I send my files?

Upload your stems or mix to WeTransfer, Google Drive or Dropbox and paste the link in the form, or send them through Fiverr. WAV at the session’s sample rate and bit depth, with nothing on the master bus.

How long does it take?

Mastering in about 48 hours, mix and master in 3 to 5 days, mix feedback within 48 hours. Albums and EPs get a schedule up front.

How many revisions are included?

Up to 5 revisions on mastering; unlimited on the mix and master package with up to 60 stems. Most projects need one or two rounds.

How loud will my master be?

As loud as the music allows without damage. For streaming I keep the true peak at −0.3 or −0.2 dBTP and set the loudness by genre; club masters usually run louder. You can check any version with the free LUFS meter.

Do you also produce or arrange?

No. I only mix and master, and vocal tuning is part of the mix. Arrangement, production and edits such as radio versions come from you, ready to mix.

Can I book through Fiverr or SoundBetter?

Yes. Both profiles are linked below: same engineer, same chain.

Do you work in Italian?

Yes. Italian, English, German and Spanish.
