## How the money is split

Subscriptions and ads go into one pool. Spotify keeps around 30% and the rest goes to labels, distributors and publishers according to how much each track was played: if a song accounts for 1% of streams in a period, it receives 1% of the pool. This is the pro rata model.

The money does not go straight to the artist: it passes through the label or distributor, under their own contract.

## The 1,000-stream threshold

Since 2024 a track must pass 1,000 streams in the previous 12 months, from a minimum number of unique listeners, to share in recording royalties. Spotify does not disclose that minimum. The threshold does not apply to publishing royalties for songwriters and publishers, which are still paid.

## So, how much?

| Streams | Estimate at $0.004 |
|---|---|
| 1,000 | about $4 |
| 100,000 | about $400 |
| 1,000,000 | about $4,000 |

It is a rough estimate, before the label or distributor takes its share. The real figure is only in your statements. According to figures released by Spotify, more than 13,800 artists earned at least $100,000 from the platform in 2025.
