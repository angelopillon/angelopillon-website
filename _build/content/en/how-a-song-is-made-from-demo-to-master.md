<div class="tldr"><span class="box__title">In short</span>
<ul>
<li>There are six stages: writing, production, recording, mixing, mastering, release.</li>
<li>Mixing balances the instruments against each other; mastering prepares the finished track for the world.</li>
<li>Each stage depends on the one before: a problem left behind costs more later.</li>
</ul></div>

I work in the last two technical stages, mixing and mastering. Tracks reach me in every possible state, and the difference between a song that takes off and one that struggles is often decided long before it gets to me.

## 1. Writing

Melody, chords, lyrics. A good song holds up with just a guitar. If it does not work in that form, no sound will save it.

## 2. Production

This is where the outfit is chosen: which instruments, which groove, which mood. The producer builds the arrangement and picks the sounds.

## 3. Recording

Vocals and instruments are recorded. Capturing the performance well is what counts: the right mic and a good room are worth more than any fix later.

## 4. Mixing

The mix engineer puts every element in its place: level, tone, space, depth. It is the moment separate tracks become one song. More in the article on [the difference between mixing and mastering](/mixing-vs-mastering).

## 5. Mastering

The final quality check. The master refines the tone, sets the right level for the platforms and makes sure the track sounds good on headphones, in the car and in a club. On an album, it makes all the tracks consistent.

## 6. Release

The final file goes to a distributor, which delivers it to Spotify, Apple Music and the other services.

| Stage | Who does it | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| Writing | songwriter | does it work with guitar and voice? |
| Production | producer | what should it sound like? |
| Recording | engineer, musicians | is the performance captured well? |
| Mixing | mix engineer | is everything in its place? |
| Mastering | mastering engineer | does it sound good everywhere? |
| Release | artist, distributor | does it reach the right people? |

<blockquote class="pull">Every stage can improve the one before. None can replace it.</blockquote>
