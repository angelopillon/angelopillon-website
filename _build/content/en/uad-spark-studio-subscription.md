<div class="tldr"><span class="box__title">In short</span>
<ul>
<li>UAD Spark Studio has been available since 15 September 2026 at $19.99 a month or $149.99 a year.</li>
<li>It includes LUNA Studio ($199 on its own), 60+ native UAD plug-ins and virtual instruments. No UA hardware needed.</li>
<li>New plug-ins are included, Capitol Studios among them. Existing Spark subscribers move to Spark Studio at no extra cost.</li>
</ul></div>

The bundle covers tracking and mixing: LUNA to record and arrange, the UAD library for the sound. It includes emulations from Neve, Lexicon, Teletronix, SSL and Studer.

## The numbers

| | Cost |
|---|---|
| Spark Studio monthly | $19.99 |
| Spark Studio yearly | $149.99 |
| LUNA Studio alone | $199 one-off |
| Capitol Studios alone | $249 one-off |

A single flagship plug-in at full price costs more than a year of subscription. But plug-ins you buy stay yours; subscription ones disappear when you stop paying, and so does the ability to reopen old sessions exactly as you left them.

## When it pays off

**Yes,** if you are building your chain and want to try everything before deciding, or if you work in LUNA.

**No,** if you use the same three or four UAD plug-ins in every session: buy them in a sale and they are yours for good.

<blockquote class="pull">If a project has to reopen in five years, the plug-ins in it have to still be there in five years.</blockquote>

A practical tip: when you send me tracks, print the effects that are part of the sound. That way the mix does not depend on what is or is not on my computer. It is all in the [stem preparation guide](/stem-preparation-guide).
