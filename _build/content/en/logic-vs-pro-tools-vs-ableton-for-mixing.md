I receive sessions from all three. The final mix happens in my studio, but the DAW a song is born in changes a lot about how the tracks arrive.

## Which one to choose

**Logic Pro** if you are on a Mac and want everything at once: the stock plug-ins are enough for a serious mix. I covered it in the article on [Logic Pro 13](/logic-pro-13-whats-new).

**Pro Tools** if you work with other studios or want to engineer: audio editing is still the fastest, and sessions move between studios without surprises. What is new in 2026.11 is [here](/pro-tools-2026-11-stem-separator).

**Ableton Live** if you produce electronic music: for writing and arranging it is unbeatable. It mixes perfectly well as long as you keep your gain staging tidy. I covered 12.4 [here](/ableton-live-12-4-link-audio).

<blockquote class="pull">The best DAW for mixing is the one you know so well you stop thinking about it.</blockquote>
