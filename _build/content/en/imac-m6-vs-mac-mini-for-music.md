Apple announced the M6 iMac on 27 October 2026, skipping the M5 generation. The design is unchanged; it gains Wi-Fi 7 through the N1 chip, faster SSDs and an option with a 2.5 Gb Ethernet port on the power adapter. Prices are US prices.

## What matters in a studio

**Placement.** Between the two monitors you want the listening triangle, not a big screen bouncing sound back at your ears. With a Mac mini you put the computer anywhere and pick the screen, lower or further back if needed.

**Ports.** Audio interface, sample drives, controllers: they fill up fast. The two-port iMac is tight for a studio; if you go iMac, get the four-port model. If you use Thunderbolt interfaces such as the [Audient Horizon](/audient-horizon), always check compatibility.

**Memory.** For sessions with many tracks and heavy plug-ins, memory matters more than cores. Prefer more RAM over more CPU.

<blockquote class="pull">The right studio computer is the one you neither hear nor see while you listen.</blockquote>

My pick for anyone who mixes and masters: a Mac mini with as much RAM as you can afford, and the money saved on the screen spent on monitors and acoustic treatment. More on that in the article on [home studio budget](/home-studio-budget).
