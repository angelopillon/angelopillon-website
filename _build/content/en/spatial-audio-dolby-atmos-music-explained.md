<ul>
<li>Spatial audio places sounds around and above the listener, not just between two speakers.</li>
<li>Apple Music has offered spatial audio with Dolby Atmos since June 2021 at no extra cost; supported tracks carry a badge.</li>
<li>An Atmos mix is a new mix, separate from the stereo one.</li>

In stereo every sound lives on a line between the left and right speaker. With Dolby Atmos the engineer can place each element as an object in three-dimensional space: in front, behind, above.

## How you listen

**On headphones.** The system simulates space with processing that mimics how our ears receive sound from different directions. On Apple Music it works with AirPods, Beats and other headphones.

**On speakers.** You need compatible systems, such as Dolby Atmos soundbars or home cinema setups.

**On the platforms.** On Apple Music, tracks available in Dolby Atmos are marked with a badge, and there are dedicated playlists.

## Atmos and stereo are not the same thing

An Atmos track is not the stereo version “widened”: it is a new mix built from the original tracks. Some songs gain a lot; others feel scattered when space is used just to impress.

| | Stereo | Dolby Atmos |
|---|---|---|
| Space | between two speakers | around and above |
| Mix | one | a new, separate one |
| Listening | anywhere | headphones or compatible systems |

<blockquote class="pull">Space is an effect until it serves the song. Then it becomes music.</blockquote>
