I deliver masters to artists who are about to release all the time. This is the checklist I give everyone, in the right order.

## 1. Finish the track

Mix and master must be final before you upload. Changing the file after release means releasing it again. Deliver the master as WAV, in the format your distributor asks for: more in the article on [master delivery formats](/master-delivery-formats).

## 2. Pick a distributor

DistroKid, CD Baby, TuneCore and many others deliver your track to every service. Prices, fees and extras differ. The distributor also assigns the ISRC, the code that identifies the recording.

## 3. Prepare the material

- **Artwork**: square, at the resolution your distributor requires, with no logos or text that is not allowed.
- **Credits**: writers, producers, performers, spelled correctly.
- **Metadata**: title, genre, language, explicit flag if needed.

## 4. Set the date

Upload at least three or four weeks before release. Your distributor needs time, and so do you, to pitch the song.

## 5. Pitch it to playlists

In Spotify for Artists you can pitch one unreleased song at a time to the editorial playlist curators. Pitch at least 7 days before release and the song lands in your followers’ Release Radar. A pitch does not guarantee placement, but it costs nothing.

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| 4-6 weeks before | master finished, artwork ready |
| 3-4 weeks before | upload to the distributor |
| at least 7 days before | pitch in Spotify for Artists |
| release day | share, say thanks, watch the data |

<blockquote class="pull">Release day is prepared a month earlier.</blockquote>
