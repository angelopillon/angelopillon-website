Three ways to work together.

We always start from what you have: a demo, a voice and guitar sketch or a session already under way.

  • 01

    Arrangement

    I start from your demo, even just voice and guitar or voice and piano. I define structure, harmony, voicings and the role of every instrument, then I play and record the parts.

  • 02

    Full production

    The track from idea to master: arrangement, guitars, piano and keys, synths and programmed drums, editing, then mix and master through my hybrid analog chain.

    Send me the session or a stereo bounce with tempo and key. You get back doubled rhythm parts, arpeggios, lines, solos and textures as separate WAV tracks, with a clean DI of every part.

How I build guitars into an arrangement.

  • Real doubles, never copies.

    Rhythm parts panned left and right are two separate takes, often on different guitars or pickups. Real width, and no phase trouble when the track folds to mono.

  • Voicings that leave room.

    Inversions and triads up the neck instead of full open chords: the bass stays clean and the vocal keeps its space. Piano taught me to think in voices, not in chord shapes.

  • A register for every part.

    Rhythm parts in the low mids, arpeggios and textures up high, lines and leads in the gaps the vocal leaves. Every guitar is heard without turning anything up.

  • The right pickup for the role.

    Humbucker for sustain and body, single coil for definition and attack, P90 for crunch with air. The choice is made at the source, not in the mix.

  • Tuning and intonation.

    Fresh strings, checked intonation, tuning verified on every take. A guitar drifting flat in the chorus cannot be fixed with EQ.

  • Phase and mono under control.

    When a guitar goes through DI and microphone, the two signals are time-aligned and the polarity is checked. Every double is checked in mono before delivery.
