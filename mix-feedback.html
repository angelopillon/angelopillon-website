What is in the report

A PDF with the verdict first: what works, what does not, and what I would change, in order of priority. Each note has a timestamp, so you know exactly where to listen. Spectrum and LUFS analysis support what the ears say, they do not replace it.

What I listen for

Low end and the relationship between kick and bass. Midrange separation and vocal level. Stereo image, phase and mono compatibility. Depth, dynamics and how the mix translates from studio monitors to a phone and a car.

Who it is for

Producers who mix their own music and want a second pair of trained ears before release or before mastering. If the report says the mix is ready, mastering is the next step. If it is not, you know what to fix and why.

What you get

  • PDF report with fixes in order of priority
  • Notes with timestamps
  • Spectrum and LUFS analysis
  • A clear verdict: ready for mastering or not

What I need from you

  • Your stereo mix in WAV
  • One or two reference tracks
  • The points you are unsure about

How a project runs

  1. Send your files

    Stems or a stereo mix as WAV, with the tempo and a short note about the track. I check everything before I start.

  2. Brief and references

    Tell me how it should feel and where it is going: reference tracks, platforms, club or streaming.

  3. Mix and master

    The session runs through the hybrid chain. You get a first version with notes on what I changed and why.

  4. Revisions and delivery

    Ask for any change. When it is right you receive the final files for streaming, club and download.
