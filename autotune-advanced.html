Antares launches AutoTune Advanced: hard tuning, vibrato intact

Read inItalianoEnglishDeutsch

The promise is as old as AutoTune: fix the pitch without removing the singer’s personality. This time there is an engine dedicated to vibrato.

By Angelo Pillon 9 November 2026 3 min read
On this page

On this page

  1. What’s new
  2. Which one to choose
  3. What it means for the mix
  4. Sources
In short
  • AutoTune Advanced was released in late September 2026 and now tops Antares’ automatic range.
  • The DVA engine recognises the singer’s natural vibrato and keeps it, even with hard correction. Live mode runs under 1 ms of latency.
  • It costs $450 as a perpetual licence. Graph Mode and harmonies stay on AutoTune Pro 11.

AutoTune has always been accused of flattening vocals: when correction is fast, vibrato turns into a straight line. AutoTune Advanced is built to fix exactly that.

What’s new

  • DVA: a patent-pending vibrato engine that tells a pitch error from a singer’s choice. Vibrato can be preserved, reduced or emphasised.
  • Live mode: under 1 ms of latency from 44.1 to 192 kHz, useful for tracking and live shows.
  • Formant and transpose, a 78-key visual keyboard, MIDI control through MIDI Perform and AutoKey 2 integration.
  • Signature presets from producers and engineers including Ann Mincieli, Bart Schoudel and David “Yungin” Kim.

Which one to choose

Edition For Price
AutoTune (2026) everyday automatic correction $300
AutoTune Pro 11 manual editing in Graph Mode, harmonies $550
AutoTune Advanced natural automatic correction, live $450

All of them are included in the AutoTune Unlimited subscription.

What it means for the mix

In rap and trap, AutoTune is part of the sound and I treat it as an effect. In pop and R&B it needs to be invisible, and that is where preserved vibrato makes a huge difference. One tip that applies to every version: always send me the untuned vocal too. If the printed tuning does not convince, I can redo it. More in the article on rap and trap vocals with AutoTune.
