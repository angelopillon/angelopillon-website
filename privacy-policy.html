Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated: November 2026

This website is run by Angelo Pillon, a mixing and mastering engineer working as a sole trader in Milan, Italy. This page explains what personal data the site processes, why, and what your rights are under the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679).

The short version: the audio tools work entirely on your computer, the contact form sends me only what you type in it, and traffic statistics are collected only if you accept them.

1. Data controller

Angelo Pillon (sole proprietorship Pillon Angelo Alberto, VAT number IT01981550930), Milan, Italy. Email: angelopillon@gmail.com

2. Contact form

When you send the contact form, I receive the data you enter: your name, your email address, the service you are interested in, the link to your files if you add one, your message, and the language of the page you sent it from.

  • Purpose: to reply to your request, prepare a quote and, if we work together, manage the project.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and my legitimate interest in answering messages I receive (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Processor: the form is handled by Netlify Forms, part of the hosting service of Netlify, Inc. Submissions are stored in my Netlify account and forwarded to my email.
  • Retention: as long as needed to handle your request and, if no project follows, no longer than 2 years. If we work together, the data needed for invoicing is kept for the period required by Italian tax law.

The files you share through a link (WeTransfer, Google Drive, Dropbox or similar) are governed by the terms of that service. I use them only to work on your project.

3. Audio tools

The LUFS meter, the stereo analyzer, the A/B player and the other tools process audio files entirely in your browser with the Web Audio API. Your files are not uploaded, not stored and not sent to any server. The tools collect no personal data.

4. Analytics, only with your consent

If you click Accept on the cookie notice, the site loads Google Analytics 4 to collect statistics on how it is used: pages visited, time spent, device type and approximate country or city. I use them to understand which pages and tools are useful. Advertising features are not enabled, and the data is not used to show you ads.

  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with the Cookie settings button at the bottom of every page.
  • Provider: Google Ireland Limited, with possible transfer of data to Google LLC in the United States under the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_<ID>, which last up to 2 years.

If you click Decline, or don’t choose, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookie is set.

5. What the site stores on your device

Name What it is Why Duration
ap_consent Local storage Remembers whether you accepted or declined statistics Until you change it or clear your browser data
Service worker cache Browser cache Keeps a copy of the site’s files so the tools work offline Until the next update of the site or until you clear it
_ga, _ga_<ID> Cookies, only with consent Google Analytics statistics Up to 2 years

The first two are technically necessary for features you use and contain no personal data. They are never sent to third parties.

6. Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. To deliver the pages and protect the service, Netlify processes technical data such as your IP address, the page requested and your browser type in its server logs. This happens on the basis of my legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)). Netlify is based in the United States; transfers take place under the safeguards provided by the GDPR.

7. Spotify player

The home page includes a Spotify player with a selection of tracks I mixed and mastered. The player loads when you reach that section of the page: from that moment Spotify AB processes data about your visit and may set its own cookies, under Spotify’s privacy policy.

8. Fonts, scripts, links and sharing

Fonts, scripts and animation libraries are served from this site: no request is made to Google Fonts or to other external services when the pages load. The site loads no social media plugins. Links to Fiverr, SoundBetter, WhatsApp, Telegram or LinkedIn, including the share buttons on articles, only take you to those services when you click them; from that moment, their privacy policies apply.

9. Who receives your data

Your data is never sold. It is processed only by the providers named on this page, acting as processors: Netlify, Inc. for hosting and the contact form, Google for analytics if you accept them, and Spotify if you load the player.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format and withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before. To exercise these rights, write to angelopillon@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

11. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Form submissions are encrypted in transit.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. The Italian version of this policy is available at /it/privacy.
