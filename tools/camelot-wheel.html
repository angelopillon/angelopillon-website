Interactive Camelot wheel

Choose a key on the wheel. Compatible keys light up, with the move that gets you there.

CodeKeyMove

How the wheel works

The Camelot system gives every key a code from 1 to 12, with A for minor and B for major. Neighbouring codes share almost all their notes, so mixing between them sounds natural. Click a key on the wheel and the compatible ones light up, with the kind of move:

  • Same code: the same key, the smoothest mix.
  • One step either way, for example from 8A to 7A or 9A: the safest change.
  • Same number, other letter, from 8A to 8B: the relative major or minor, a change of mood.
  • Two steps up: an energy boost that works over a few bars.
  • Up a semitone: a dramatic lift, best on a breakdown.

Finding the key of a track

If you don’t know the key, the LUFS meter estimates it from the file, with a confidence level. Key detection on dense electronic music is good, not perfect: confirm it by ear against the bass line.

For producers too

The wheel isn’t only for DJs. Writing in a key that sits next to the keys your favourite DJs play makes your track easier to mix, and the wheel helps you choose the key of a remix or an edit. More in The Camelot wheel and harmonic mixing.
